# The synthetic sample dataset

The tool opens on a made-up dataset so every view works before anything real is loaded. It is generated by `src/sample.js` from a fixed seed, so the files are identical every time; `node scripts/make_sample.js` writes them to `data/sample/`, and the Data tab downloads the same files. **Nothing in it is CAVA's real cost, sales or margin data.**

## What is real and what is invented

| Real (from the public menu, read Oct 16, 2026) | Invented |
|---|---|
| The 40 curated items, sides, drinks and desserts at the Dupont Circle store and their prices | Every cost |
| The 44 build-your-own components, their stations and up-charges | Portions used in curated recipes |
| Menu calories, and per-portion nutrition from CAVA's nutrition guide | Three stores, their names and their order volumes |
| The $11.75 build-your-own base | Every order, build, waste row, inventory count and P&L figure |

## Parameters and the ranges behind them

Costs are placed inside ranges a food-cost manager would recognise, not taken from any supplier. Case sizes and units are chosen so the case-to-portion math is exercised (weight, volume and count cases; yields from 72% to 100%).

| Group | Portion | Cost per portion (sample) | Typical published range for fast casual |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greens | 2–3 oz | $0.48–0.58 | $0.35–0.75 |
| Grains and lentils | 1.5–1.8 oz raw | $0.12–0.17 | $0.10–0.30 |
| Dips and spreads | 2 fl oz | $0.20–0.45 | $0.15–0.50 |
| Included mains (chicken, falafel, roasted vegetables) | 4 oz / 3 pieces | $0.68–1.02 | $0.60–1.40 |
| Premium mains (steak, lamb, salmon) | 3.5–4 oz | $1.47–3.86 ($4.33 for salmon from September) | $1.50–4.50 |
| Toppings | 1–2.5 oz | $0.07–0.33; avocado $0.65 per half | $0.05–0.80 |
| Dressings | 1.5 fl oz | $0.22–0.40 | $0.15–0.50 |
| Pita, chips | 1 each / 3 oz | $0.30, $0.58 | $0.20–0.70 |
| House drinks, bottled drinks, desserts | per serving | $0.18–1.55 | $0.15–1.75 |

Resulting theoretical food cost: 26–28% of sales before paper, 29–30% with paper, in the 28–33% band usually quoted for fast casual.

Other parameters (all in `src/sample.js`):

- **Stores**: DC-001 Dupont Circle (140 orders a day on a weekday), MD-014 Bethesda (110, bowls and pitas $0.30 cheaper), VA-022 Clarendon (120, with a regional item sold nowhere else). Weekday/weekend profiles differ by store; July = 1.00, August = 0.96, September = 1.04.
- **Channels**: in-store 52–58%, app 18–22%, web 8–9%, delivery 16–17%. Delivery prices carry a 12% markup; 6% of app orders have a 10% discount (so `net_price` differs from the menu).
- **Dayparts**: lunch 55%, dinner 30%, afternoon 10%, before 11:00 5%.
- **Orders**: 1 meal (72%), 2 (20%), 3 (6%), 4 (2%); delivery orders are larger. 62% of meals are build-your-own (bowls 80%, pitas 20%), 38% curated with set popularity weights.
- **Builds**: 70% come from 13 archetype builds (some mirror curated items, some exist only on the line), each with a 25% chance of one topping swapped; 30% are random picks with per-station weights, including doubles (2%), half-and-half mains (5%) and the free quarter pita (55% of bowls). Low-pick components by design: baby spinach, braised lamb, balsamic date vinaigrette.
- **Add-ons**: chips 17–22% (with a dip 45% of the time), side dip 7%, drink 18–34% by channel, dessert 11–15%, kids drink 3%.
- **Cost versions**: on 2026-09-01 salmon +12%, avocado −8%, grilled chicken +4%, harissa honey chicken +4%.
- **Packaging**: bowls $0.32 dine-in / $0.61 digital; pitas $0.18 / $0.47; sides, drinks, desserts smaller.
- **Waste log**: weekly, per store and perishable component, 1–4% of theoretical usage by family, plus a par-driven floor for the low movers (so they show waste far above their use).
- **Books (actuals)**: food cost 6–7.5% above theoretical by store (a 1.6–2.0 point gap, inside the pass band), except Clarendon in September at 21% above (a 5.7 point gap: a deliberate Fail).
- **Inventory counts**: twelve components with built-in variances (feta +14%, pickled onions +12%, avocado +9%, salmon +8%, rice −3%).

## Size and speed

92 days × 3 stores: about 33,000 orders, 74,000 sales lines and 267,000 modifier rows. The page builds the dataset in about 0.6 s and analyses it in under a second on a laptop. A real single store-month is a tenth of that. Pass `scale` to `makeSample` for larger tests.

## Regenerating

```
node scripts/make_sample.js
```

writes `data/templates/`, `docs/data-dictionary.md` and `data/sample/`. The two large files (`sales.csv`, `modifiers.csv`) are not committed; one store-week excerpts of each are.
