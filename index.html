Overview

  • Unofficial. This is an independent fan project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to CAVA Group, Sweetgreen, Chipotle or their restaurants. Brand and menu-item names belong to their owners.
  • Menus and prices change. Prices come from each chain's online ordering menu for one downtown Washington, DC store, read Oct 16, 2026: CAVA Dupont Circle (1222 Connecticut Ave NW), Sweetgreen Farragut Square (888 17th St NW) and Chipotle Dupont Circle (1300 Connecticut Ave NW). Other locations charge differently. Calories are as printed on each menu. Sweetgreen's bottled drinks and sweets aren't priced on its store menu, so they're shown with “—”.
  • Sources: cava.com/menu and its bowl builder (every build-your-own component with its up-charge) plus CAVA's Nutrition & Allergen Guide PDF; order.sweetgreen.com and sweetgreen.com/menu; chipotle.com's online ordering menu (featured items, the High Protein menu, chips & sides, drinks). The brands and menu lines covered:
    • What counts as an item: each chain's named, fixed-recipe items (curated bowls, pitas, salads, wraps, plates, burritos, kids meals) plus sides, drinks and desserts. Build-your-own orders have no fixed recipe, so they're left out; CAVA's build-your-own line is kept as a station catalog instead (see Operations → Prep & inventory and Menu gaps). Chipotle's regular burritos, bowls, tacos and salads are all build-your-own, so only its named items appear.
    • Each ingredient line on a menu is mapped to one component. Generic produce merges across brands (cucumber is cucumber everywhere), while named preparations stay distinct (CAVA's grilled chicken, Sweetgreen's blackened chicken and Chipotle's adobo chicken are three components). Plain “rice” at CAVA counts as saffron basmati rice, and a side dip's description maps to the dip itself. Drink and dessert components come from the menu descriptions.
    • Bowls, pitas, salads, wraps, plates, burritos and kids meals are meals; sides, chips, drinks and desserts are add-ons. Vegetarian means no chicken, beef, pork, lamb or fish.
    • The “Cost & sales” tool opens on a synthetic sample dataset: made-up costs inside published fast-casual ranges, CAVA's real menu structure and prices, and seeded random sales for three imaginary stores over three months. None of it is CAVA's real cost, sales or margin data. Loading your own files replaces the sample; the files are parsed and computed in the browser only, with no upload and no storage unless you opt in. Method, data dictionary and security notes are in the docs folder of the repository.
    • Operations views use the menus only. Sales and cost data aren't included. Menu gaps compare whichever menu lines are selected; Compare brands and Head to head always use every brand.
    • A frequent pairing means the menu uses it often. It doesn't rank taste or nutrition.
