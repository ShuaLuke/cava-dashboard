Overview

  • Unofficial. This is an independent fan project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to CAVA Group, Inc. or its restaurants. CAVA, Crazy Feta and the other names belong to their owners.
  • Menus and prices change. Prices come from CAVA's online ordering menu for the Dupont Circle store (1222 Connecticut Ave NW, Washington, DC), read Oct 16, 2026. Other locations charge differently. Calories are as printed on the menu.
  • Source: cava.com/menu and the bowl builder on cava.com (every build-your-own component with its up-charge), plus CAVA's Nutrition & Allergen Guide PDF for per-component nutrition. The menu lines covered:
    • What counts as an item: the chef-curated bowls and pitas, the sides, drinks and desserts. Build-your-own bowls, pitas and the kids meal have no fixed components, so they live in the station catalog instead (see Prep & inventory and Menu gaps).
    • Each component line on the menu is mapped to one station component, so “corn” and “fire-roasted corn” count the same, plain “rice” counts as saffron basmati rice, and a side dip's description maps to the dip itself rather than to chickpeas and tahini. Drink and dessert components come from the menu descriptions.
    • Bowls and pitas are meals; sides, drinks and desserts are add-ons. Vegetarian means no chicken, steak, lamb or salmon.
    • The “Cost & sales” tab uses made-up, adjustable cost assumptions and a random simulated sales sample to show what real cost and POS data would make possible. None of those numbers are CAVA's real costs, sales or margins.
    • Operations views use the menu only. Sales and cost data aren't included. Menu gaps compare whichever menu lines are selected.
    • A frequent pairing means the menu uses it often. It doesn't rank taste or nutrition.
